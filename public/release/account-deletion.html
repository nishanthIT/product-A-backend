Delete your Paymi account

Paymi is operated by Kaliam. You can request deletion here without reinstalling the app. Verify your own account with your email and password.

Deletion removes your account, memberships, personal lists and authored chat content. You will lose access. Shared shops, other staff accounts and shared operational records remain. Personal details in shared records and external services require review before the request is complete. Workspace deletion is a separate authorized action.

Keep your private receipt to check progress. A request is not confirmation that deletion has finished. Contact support@paymi.co.uk about a request or inaccessible account. Never email your password.
